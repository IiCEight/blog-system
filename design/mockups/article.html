A small note is
enough to begin.

Write down the thought before you understand it completely.

CREATEDOctober 01, 2026READING TIME3 minutesLearning
IN THIS NOTE
Start with a sentenceLeave room to returnKeep the system simple

Most useful notes begin before the idea is ready. A sentence, an observation, a question you haven't answered yet.

The value isn't in making every entry complete. It's in keeping something you can think with later.

Start with a sentence

When an idea appears, give it somewhere to live. Don't make a title, a folder, or an outline a prerequisite for writing.

Capture the thought while it is yours.
You can give it structure tomorrow.

A useful starting point can be very small:

  • What did I notice?
  • What surprised me?
  • What would I like to understand next?

Leave room to return

A note can change without losing its beginning. Keep the original date, add the new explanation, and let the record show how your understanding developed.

MomentWhat to keep
First thoughtA sentence and a date
Later understandingAn explanation or example
A new connectionA link to another note

Keep the system simple

The original note can remain a plain Markdown file. The website gives it a comfortable place to be read.
