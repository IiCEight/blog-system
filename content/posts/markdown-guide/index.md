## Mathematics

Inline expressions such as $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ render without contacting an external service.

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

Use dollar delimiters for math; avoid unescaped dollar signs used as currency in the same paragraph.
